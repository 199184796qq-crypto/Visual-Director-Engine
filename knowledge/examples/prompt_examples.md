# Example 01
# Cinematic Character Portrait
# 电影人物肖像


## User Input


一个40岁的东方男人，雨夜街头，很压抑。



================================================

# Visual Analysis


Subject:

40-year-old East Asian man


Emotion:

loneliness,
pressure,
silence


Environment:

urban street at night,
rainy atmosphere



================================================

# Visual Direction


Style:

realistic cinematic drama


Reference:

Hong Kong cinema emotional atmosphere



================================================

# Universal Prompt


Concept:

A melancholic cinematic portrait of a man lost in a rainy urban night.



Subject:

A 40-year-old East Asian man with a serious expression,
wearing a dark coat,
standing alone on a wet street.



Environment:

Old city street,
neon reflections on wet pavement,
soft rain falling in the background.



Composition:

Medium shot,
subject positioned slightly off-center,
large negative space creating loneliness.



Camera:

35mm cinematic lens,
shallow depth of field,
ARRI Alexa style photography.



Lighting:

Mixed neon and soft street lighting,
cool shadows,
warm highlights.



Color:

Muted blue-green palette,
subtle warm orange accents.



Material:

Realistic skin texture,
wet fabric,
rain reflections.



Mood:

Melancholic,
quiet,
cinematic.



================================================

# MJ Version


A 40-year-old East Asian man standing alone on a rainy city street,
wearing a dark coat,
neon reflections on wet pavement,
melancholic Hong Kong cinema atmosphere,
35mm cinematic photography,
ARRI Alexa style,
moody lighting,
film grain

--ar 16:9 --style raw



================================================

# Flux Version


A realistic cinematic photograph showing a 40-year-old East Asian man standing alone on a rainy urban street at night. Neon lights reflect on the wet pavement while soft street lighting creates a melancholic atmosphere. Shot with a 35mm cinematic lens.
